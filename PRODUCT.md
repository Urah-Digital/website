## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML pages with client-side JavaScript, deployed on Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

Directors of Nigerian schools, owners and leaders of professional service firms, and established online brands seeking a trustworthy digital presence.

## Product Purpose

URAH DIGITAL designs and builds high-precision websites for organizations whose reputation is a primary asset. The website explains the studio's approach and helps prospective clients start a direct project conversation.

## Positioning

A selective web studio focused on trust, clear scopes, milestone-based delivery, direct senior engagement, and client ownership of digital assets.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients review the website as part of deciding whether to contact the studio. WhatsApp and email are the primary contact channels. Website content is maintained in the Git repository and published through the static-site deployment workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static HTML project with no application server, database, or authenticated admin interface.
- Public content is maintained in version-controlled JSON. Repository access and review control who can publish changes.
