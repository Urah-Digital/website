# Product Strategy: URAH DIGITAL

## 1. Core Positioning
- **Name:** URAH DIGITAL
- **Tagline / Positioning:** "Websites that earn trust."
- **Core Promise:** Selective web studio for Nigerian schools, service businesses, and established online brands whose reputation is their product. Premium, calm, precise—engineered for trust, clarity, and milestone protection.
- **Formality / Tone:** Formal, respectful, calm, and senior advisor persona ("Good afternoon / Good morning"). Clear, exact, and proof-driven.

## 2. Target Audience & Visitor Mindset
- **Primary Audience:** Directors of Nigerian schools, owners of professional service businesses (law firms, consultancies, medical practices), and established online brands.
- **Visitor Context:** They are busy, serious buyers who have likely been burned by vague quotes, delayed projects, or cheap template builders.

## 5. Design Direction & Inspiration Synthesis
### Palette
- **Primary Surface:** Obsidian Black (`#0F0F0F` / `#1A1A1A`) — Void-like, high-end, velvet dark canvas.
- **Accent & Trust Anchor:** Warm Gold / Brass (`#C8A962`) — Used with restraint for subtle accents, key focal points, and badges.
- **Light Surface / Inverted Blocks:** Off-White / Cream (`#F9F9F9`) — Applied for intentional contrast sections (inspired by Wise's rhythmic section inversion).
- **Text:** Slate / Charcoal (`#2B2B2B`) on light surfaces; Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) / Bone (`#F0F0F0`) on dark surfaces.
- **Hairlines:** Subtle Graphite (`#292D30` or `#262626`) 1px borders defining cards, inputs, and structure without drop shadows.

### Typography
- **Hero & Key Headlines:** Editorial Serif Display (GT Sectra / Playfair Display feel) with tight tracking to signal institutional weight and senior credibility.
- **Body & Structural Copy:** Clean, highly legibly Modern Sans (Inter / Söhne) for maximum legibility across pricing tables, scopes, and descriptions.
- **Proof & Metadata:** Monospaced accent font (Commit Mono / JetBrains Mono) for scope metrics, pricing tags, milestone dates, and live status indicators.

### Component & Surface Architecture (Inspiration Blend)
- **From Resend:** Pure dark canvas baseline, 1px hairline graphite borders for separation, pill badges for announcements/status, dark product/case proof cards, zero drop shadows.
- **From Wise:** High-contrast section inversions (switching cleanly between dark obsidian and warm off-white), confident block display headlines, high-impact CTA pill geometry.
